PROCESS

How a commission works

Four steps from request to delivered emote.

  • 1

    Send a request

    Tell me your character, how many emotes, and any references.

  • 2

    Sketch & quote

    You'll see a rough sketch and confirmed price before final art starts.

  • 3

    Revisions

    2 minor revisions are included once the sketch is approved.

  • 4

    Delivery

    Final files sent once payment is complete, ready to upload.

TERMS

Terms & conditions

Please read through before reaching out for a commission.

💳 Payment

  • PayPal is the only accepted payment method.
  • Partial payment (50% or less of the total) is available only for full illustrations priced above $50, due once the draft is approved.

📦 Delivery

  • Finished pieces are sent via social platforms once payment is complete.
  • Delivery by email is available on request.

↩️ Refunds

  • No refunds once a commission is completed at the customer's request.
  • If a piece is left incomplete, a partial refund of 50% of the original price may be requested with valid reason.
